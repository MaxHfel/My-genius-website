My Genius: Startups

Building new things to solve real problems

Why Startups?

I have always had a passion for innovation. I love coming up with new ideas, creating things, and building them from the ground up. Over time, I realized that I could combine these passions and use them to do good in the world. Startups let me do exactly that: turn an idea into something real that solves a problem and helps people.

How It Started

When I was young, I was always making and building new things. Whenever I noticed a problem around the house, I tried to invent a solution for it. Those small projects taught me how to think creatively, test ideas, and keep improving until something actually worked.

How It's Going

Later, I teamed up with friends to build HydraSmart, our first real startup. We competed in TYE PNW and earned 3rd place, which showed me that our ideas could hold up against many teams of strong competition, as well as impress a panel of judges that included people such as one of Bill Gates's advisors.

Now, I am working on building another startup alongside a professor from UC Berkeley. Each project teaches me something new about turning ideas into real products, and I am excited to keep building.

My New Startup: InSeason

Developing an app and database to optimize regional food systems around seasonality, cutting carbon emissions from transport and overproduction. The platform builds local ecosystems connecting consumers and producers to promote healthy eating and strengthen sustainable agriculture while cultivating culinary cultures which are being lost.
